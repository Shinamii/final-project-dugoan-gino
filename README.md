# Build Your Own Bite
Build Your Own Bite is a web-based noodle ordering system designed to let customers customize their noodle meals and 
conveniently place orders for delivery, dine-in, or takeout.

## Problem
Customers in Manolo Fortich experience long waiting lines and confusing food orders, 
especially during peek hours.

Build Your Own Bite helps customers customize and place their food orders easily through 
a web-based system.

## Target Users
Delivery Customer; Walk-in Customer

## Solution
Build Your Own Bite provides an easy-to-use online noodle ordering system where customers can choose their preferred 
noodle varieties, customize their spice levels and add-ons, and select their payment method. It simplifies the ordering 
process, reduces confusion between customers and cashiers, and minimizes long waiting lines for a more convenient 
ordering experience.

## Main Feature
Customers can browse different noodle varieties, customize their preferred spice levels and add-ons, and conveniently 
place their orders online.


## Pages
- Homepage: Homepage: If you are new to our web application, you can sign up or log in 
 to your account.
- App page: Customers can choose their order type, noodles, spice level, and add-ons
They can also view prices and check their cart before checkout.
- Cart Page: On this page, you can view the products you have selected, including their 
quantities. You can also update or remove items.
- Order Summary Page: Customers can review their selected food items, quantities, and
total price, choose their payment method (online payment or Cash on Delivery), and 
confirm their order.
- Delivery Tracking Page: Customers can track their order status, from processing and 
preparing to out for delivery and delivered.
